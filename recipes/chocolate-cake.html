Chocolate Cake

Description

Chocolate cake is a delicious dessert made with cocoa powder, flour, sugar, eggs, and other ingredients. It is soft, rich, and perfect for dessert.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Cocoa powder
  • Sugar
  • Eggs
  • Milk
  • Butter
  • Baking powder

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven.
  2. Mix the flour, cocoa powder, sugar, and baking powder.
  3. Add the eggs, milk, and butter.
  4. Mix everything until the batter is smooth.
  5. Pour the batter into a cake pan.
  6. Bake until the cake is fully cooked.
  7. Let the cake cool before serving.
