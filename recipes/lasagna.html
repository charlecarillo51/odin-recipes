Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a delicious Italian dish made with layers of pasta, meat sauce, cheese, and other ingredients.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Cheese
  • Onion

Steps

  1. Cook the ground beef and onion.
  2. Add the tomato sauce.
  3. Cook the lasagna noodles.
  4. Layer the noodles, sauce, and cheese.
  5. Bake until cooked.
